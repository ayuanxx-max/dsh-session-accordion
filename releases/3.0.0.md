# dsh-session-accordion 3.0.0

## 中文

**一句话**：每一轮对话折成一行，最新一轮展开；点折叠行看内容、再点收回；钉住的那一轮永远不折。

本版（3.0.0）包含：

- **折叠/展开**：加载与会话切换后只有最新一轮展开；点任意折叠行展开该轮并折回其他轮；
  再点收回；新轮次到达时上一轮自动折回；`Esc` 全收。
- **三态配色**：折叠行=灰底次要色字，展开行=原样无底色，钉住行=左侧 2px 强调色条
  （折叠时叠淡蓝底 + 📌 高亮），一眼分得出哪些是打开的、哪些是折起来的。
- **钉住与记忆**：📌 钉住后不会被新消息折回，再点解除；按会话记在
  `localStorage['dsh-ac-mem:<会话键>']`，重开自动恢复。
- **搜索（🔍）**：常驻入口悬在**发送箭头正上方**（同 x、同尺寸）；**点击才打开**，
  **默认不打开**；搜索框开在它**左侧**、同一水平带。输入实时过滤折叠行、显示 `当前/总数`，
  `Enter` 跳下一个命中并展开，`Esc` 关闭并还原。快捷键 `Ctrl/Cmd+Shift+K` 为最佳努力
  （DSH 自带查找面板会抢 `Ctrl+Shift+F` / `Ctrl+Alt+F` / 有时抢 `Ctrl+Shift+K`）。
- **时间线**：按天插日期吸头（今天/昨天/MM-DD），hover 折叠行出预览气泡。
- **分叉徽标**：分叉会话顶部显示 `↳ 分叉 · L<深度> · <来源标题>`。
- **稳定性与性能**：永不写 `scrollTop`；滚动中只同步折叠**新增行**，流式 delta 走 rAF 节流；
  📌 几何量测滚动中限流 90ms、滚停后全量校正；签名未变的行整行跳过。
- **主题/无障碍/双语**：中英文案、`aria-*` + `aria-live`、`j`/`k` 导航、浅色主题。

回滚/降级：`__acConfig({ search: false })` 关搜索、`__acConfig({ pin: false })` 关钉住；
全部恢复默认 `localStorage.removeItem('dsh-ac-config')`。

## English

**In one line**: every chat turn folds into a single line, the newest turn stays expanded; click a
folded line to read it, click again to fold; pinned turns never fold.

What 3.0.0 ships:

- **Fold / expand**: after load or a session switch only the newest turn is expanded; clicking a
  folded line expands it and folds the previously open one; clicking the open turn folds it back;
  a new turn folds the previous one; `Esc` folds everything.
- **Three visual states**: folded = grey chip + secondary text; expanded = plain body; pinned =
  2px accent bar on the left (plus a light blue tint and a lit 📌 when folded). You can tell open,
  pinned and folded turns apart at a glance.
- **Pinning + memory**: a pinned turn is never folded away by new messages and unpins on a second
  click; state is stored per session in `localStorage['dsh-ac-mem:<session key>']`.
- **Search (🔍)**: a persistent button sits **directly above the send arrow** (same x, same size);
  the search box is **closed by default** and **opens on click**, appearing to the **left of** the
  button. Typing filters folded lines live with a `current/total` counter; `Enter` jumps to the next
  match and expands it; `Esc` closes and restores. `Ctrl/Cmd+Shift+K` is a best-effort shortcut
  (DSH's own finder grabs `Ctrl+Shift+F`, `Ctrl+Alt+F` and sometimes `Ctrl+Shift+K`).
- **Timeline**: day headers (Today / Yesterday / MM-DD) and a hover preview bubble on folded lines.
- **Fork badge**: `↳ Fork · L<depth> · <origin title>` on forked sessions.
- **Stability and performance**: never writes `scrollTop`; while scrolling only newly added rows are
  folded synchronously and streaming deltas are throttled through `requestAnimationFrame`; pin
  geometry is measured at most every 90ms during scroll and corrected once scrolling stops;
  unchanged rows are skipped.
- **Theme / a11y / i18n**: bilingual copy, `aria-*` + `aria-live`, `j`/`k` navigation, light theme.

Rollback: `__acConfig({ search: false })` to disable search, `__acConfig({ pin: false })` to disable
pinning, `localStorage.removeItem('dsh-ac-config')` to reset everything.
